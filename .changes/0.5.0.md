### Added

- **`/argus reply`: the MR author can rebut a finding.** Reply `/argus reply <reason>` in a finding's thread (or `/argus reply #<id> <reason>` on the MR for a summary-only finding; reason required, up to 500 characters). Only the MR author and assignees can use it; the reviewer is pointed to `/argus accept`. The reviewer's own engine judges the rebuttal on the reviewer's queue and budget, reading only that finding, the reason and the reviewed commit (read-only), with a 5-minute limit.
  - Rebuttal holds on a minor or nit finding: the finding is recorded as accepted via author reply, the thread is resolved and the verdict recomputed.
  - Rebuttal holds on a major or blocker finding: Argus only recommends acceptance and mentions the reviewer; the thread stays open.
  - Rebuttal does not hold, is unclear or the judgement fails: Argus explains and the finding stays open.
  - Replies are not judged when the MR head has moved past the last reviewed commit. Each comment is handled once. The AI never approves and never accepts a major or blocker on its own.
